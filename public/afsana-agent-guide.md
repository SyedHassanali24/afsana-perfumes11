# AFSANA public brand and product guide

## Purpose

This is a read-only reference for assistants that help people understand the publicly available AFSANA Perfumes website. It is a static information resource, not a shopping API, booking service, order-placement tool or customer-account interface.

## Brand

AFSANA is a Pakistan-focused perfume and fragrance brand using the identity “AFSANA / THE ART OF SCENT”. Its public website provides a perfume collection, a scent discovery experience, gifting information, brand information and fragrance journal articles.

## Where to find current information

- The live [perfume collection](https://afsanaperfumes.store/shop) is the catalogue entry point.
- Product-specific information belongs to each [product page](https://afsanaperfumes.store/shop).
- The [scent finder](https://afsanaperfumes.store/discover) helps visitors explore the collection.
- The [gifting page](https://afsanaperfumes.store/gifting) covers perfume gifting.
- The [journal](https://afsanaperfumes.store/journal) contains fragrance guides.
- Customer enquiries should be directed to [AFSANA contact](https://afsanaperfumes.store/contact).

## Accuracy and customer-safety rules

1. Read the relevant live product page before stating its current price, availability, product details or offer.
2. Do not invent perfume notes, ingredients, concentration, longevity, projection, stock scarcity, ratings or customer reviews.
3. Use the spelling shown on the relevant product page. In particular, the public route for Rose Miricle uses the spelling “Miricle”.
4. Do not claim an order has been placed, a payment has been received or an order status has been checked. The guide does not expose private customer or order data.
5. Do not represent this static guide as a live agent, MCP server, A2A endpoint or transactional service.
