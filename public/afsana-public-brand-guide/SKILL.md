# AFSANA Public Brand Guide

## Brand and scope

AFSANA is a Pakistan-focused perfume and fragrance brand using the identity “AFSANA / THE ART OF SCENT”. The public website offers a perfume collection, scent discovery, gifting information, brand information and fragrance journal articles.

This skill is a static reference for public website information. It is not a live agent, MCP server, shopping API, booking service, order-placement tool or customer-account interface.

## Use When

- Someone asks what AFSANA is or where to browse its public perfume collection.
- Someone asks where to find AFSANA's scent finder, gifting pages or fragrance guides.
- Someone needs the official website source for current public product or customer information.

## Don't Use When

- Someone asks to place an order, receive a payment, check a private order or access a customer account. Direct them to AFSANA's official website or contact page.
- Someone asks for product facts not confirmed on the current live product page. Do not guess.

## Workflow

1. Identify the public page most relevant to the question.
2. Open the live AFSANA page before stating changing details such as price, availability or offers.
3. Give the official page link so the visitor can verify the information and continue on the website.
4. Direct customer-specific requests to AFSANA's contact page.

## Rules

- Do not invent perfume notes, ingredients, concentration, longevity, projection, stock, ratings, reviews or discounts.
- Use the current spelling and details on the live product page. The public Rose Miricle route uses the spelling “Miricle”.
- Do not claim an order has been placed, a payment received or private account data checked.
- Treat this guide as read-only reference material. It does not expose transactions or private customer information.
